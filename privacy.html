Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how Otaku Quote Automation (the “Service”) may process information when you use its quote-image generation features and, if enabled and approved, TikTok integration.

1. Information that may be processed

Depending on enabled features, the Service may process quote text and related metadata, generated image files, technical logs needed to operate and troubleshoot the Service, and configuration values required to connect to third-party APIs.

If TikTok account connection is implemented, the Service may receive authorization data and account or publishing information permitted by the scopes you approve. Never share passwords or private credentials in public source code or issue trackers.

2. How information is used

Information is used to generate images and captions, operate scheduled tasks, provide requested integration features, diagnose failures, and protect the Service.

3. Third-party providers

The Service may rely on hosting and software providers, image-generation services such as Hugging Face, and TikTok APIs if enabled. Those providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Storage and security

Generated files and operational logs may be stored in the hosting environment. Access tokens and API secrets should be stored as private environment variables, never in public source code. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

5. Sharing

The Service does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by third-party providers needed to deliver enabled features, or disclosed where required by law. Content is sent to TikTok only when the integration is implemented and the required publishing action is authorized.

6. Retention and deletion

Information should be retained only as long as needed for the purposes described above. To request access to or deletion of information associated with your use of the Service, contact the operator using details published on the official website. If TikTok authorization is available, you can also revoke access through TikTok settings.

7. Children and privacy

The Service is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Do not submit sensitive personal information to the Service.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the Service changes. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact details published on the official website.

Important before submission: edit this policy so it accurately reflects your actual implementation, data storage, logs, and contact method. Add a real contact email or contact page you control. Do not submit inaccurate privacy statements to TikTok.
